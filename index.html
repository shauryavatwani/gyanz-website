The gaps stay hidden, and every tool waits to be asked.

A student who never understood how electrons are arranged in Class 9 will not ask the question that fixes Class 10 chemistry. They do not know it is missing.

Most AI tools

  • Wait for a question

Gyanz

  • Keeps a record of what you know
  • Steps back from a mistake to its root
  • Asks you to try the next step
