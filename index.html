Find the gap you never knew.

Gyanz is an AI tutor for CBSE students. It starts the learning, so you never have to know what to ask.

See the problem

A personal tutor for every student who needs one.

Many students have no private tutor, cannot afford coaching, get no help at home, or do not feel confident enough to start.

Gyanz is for them. Good guidance that knows the student, at a price a family can afford.

The students who need help most are the least likely to ask for it.

  • No private tutor

    No one is watching closely enough to notice where a student got stuck.

  • No money for coaching

    Good teaching exists, but it stays out of reach for most families.

  • No help at home

    Homework becomes a wall when nobody around can explain the next step.

  • No confidence to begin

    Not asking feels safer than asking the wrong thing.

The gaps stay hidden, and every tool waits to be asked.

A student who never understood fractions in Class 6 will not ask the question that fixes Class 9 algebra. They do not know it is missing.

Most AI tools

  • Wait for a question
  • Answer only what was asked
  • Cannot see an idea missed years ago
  • Often do the thinking for the student

Gyanz

  • Starts with one task chosen for you
  • Keeps a record of what you know
  • Steps back from a mistake to its root
  • Makes you do the thinking

How Gyanz fixes it.

The system starts.

Opening Gyanz shows one task, chosen for you. You never have to know what to ask.

It finds the root.

Every chapter is mapped to the ideas it depends on. A wrong answer sends Gyanz down that map until it finds where understanding breaks, then it fixes that first.

You do the work.

A short explanation, then your turn. Learning comes from trying, recalling and getting feedback.

Marks show it.

Progress is measured by what you can now do and checked against your school marks, not by time spent.

Built for the families who pay and the students who study.

Parents hold the key.

A parent adds the AI key and sets the spending limit. Nothing is spent without them.

Records stay on the device.

A student's progress and records are kept on their own computer.

No ads, no tracking.

Gyanz exists to help a student learn.

English and Hindi.

Ask in the language you think in, including Hinglish.

Gyanz is in pilot.

It is being tested with a Class 10 CBSE student. It starts with Classes 9 and 10 in Science, Maths and Social Science, as an app for Mac and Windows computers. It is not released yet.
